Vedic Rik Text: Inline Numerals (३ & १) and Legacy Glyphs (टट्य & ठ) Audit Report

Audits legacy OCR/typesetting artefacts where inline numeral ३ (Pluta) was encoded as टट्य, and inline numeral १ (Abhigīta/Kampa/Prolongation) was encoded as ठ.

Accent Rule Specifications Applied:
1. ठ(1) → १(2): Translates to inline numeral १ carrying the Anudātta symbol (horizontal underbar ॒). Plain ठ → १.
2. टट्य → ३: Retains and transfers any attached accents (e.g. टट्य(2) → ३(2)).
